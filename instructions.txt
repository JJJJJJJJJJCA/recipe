Galbi Beef Recipe
Ingredients
•	2 to 3 pounds flanken-style beef short ribs (cut thin across the bone)
•	½ cup soy sauce
•	¼ cup water
•	¼ cup brown sugar or honey
•	3 tablespoons minced garlic
•	1 tablespoon minced fresh ginger
•	2 tablespoons toasted sesame oil
•	½ small onion, grated or blended smooth
•	½ teaspoon black pepper
Simple Steps
1.	Rinse the ribs in cold water to remove any tiny bone fragments, then pat them completely dry with paper towels.
2.	Make the marinade by whisking the soy sauce, water, brown sugar, garlic, ginger, sesame oil, grated onion, and black pepper together in a bowl until the sugar dissolves.
3.	Marinate the meat by placing the ribs in a dish or zip-top bag, pouring the sauce over them, and coating each piece well.
4.	Refrigerate for at least 2 hours, or overnight for the best flavor and tenderness.
5.	Cook the ribs on a hot preheated grill, broiler, or a heavy skillet over medium-high heat for about 3 to 5 minutes per side until cooked through and nicely charred.
6.	Rest for 2 minutes before serving.
